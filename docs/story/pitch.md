# One-paragraph pitch (interim, Sat Oct 17)

When a card gets charged twice or a package arrives broken, getting the money back means forms, receipts, and weeks of follow-up, so a lot of people just let it go. Our project is a Fo agent that files card disputes and insurance or package claims for you and follows up until they're resolved. You paste in the email or receipt. Claude turns it into a case with the type, counterparty, amount, and evidence fields, and code decides what happens next. Before Fo contacts anyone, the agent shows you exactly who it will email, what it will say, and that it will spend nothing, and it waits for you to press Approve. Vague claims go to a human instead of being sent. Pasted text never reaches Fo's message, card numbers are masked before they're stored, double approvals still send one email, and every outbound action goes into an audit log where each entry is chained to the one before it. Tonight the Fo send is simulated and labeled on screen while we connect our Fo key. By Monday, the plan is real Fo emails to a test bank inbox and follow-ups that keep going until the case closes.
